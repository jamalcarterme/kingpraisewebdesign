## Why This Works

### Premium vs Generic
- **Generic AI Template**: Emoji + Text only
- **Premium Professional**: High-quality images + Thoughtful design

### Visual Impact
1. **Images break monotony** - Prevents that "all text" AI feel
2. **Establishes trust** - Visitors see actual digital products
3. **Improves scrolling** - Visual variety keeps engagement
4. **Professional appearance** - Stock photos signal legitimacy
5. **Better storytelling** - Services shown, not just described

## Performance Notes
- Images are **externally hosted** (no file size added)
- **Lazy loading** works automatically in modern browsers
- **Responsive** - scales beautifully on all devices
- **SEO-friendly** - alt text included on all images
- **Fast CDN delivery** - Unsplash uses global CDN
